## Finding What to Write About

The input layer. Everything below this section produces content; this section decides whether it is worth producing.

- [IntelCue](https://www.intelcue.ai) - Monitors competitors and market sources (newsletters, blogs, news, ads, patents, filings, new subdomains) and turns what changed into weekly content opportunities with a draft attached.
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/) - Free search interest over time and by region, with rising related queries.
- [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com) - Emerging trends detected across the web and curated by people to filter out fads.
- [Glimpse](https://meetglimpse.com) - Search-based trend detection with volume, growth and channel breakdowns.
- [BuzzSumo](https://buzzsumo.com) - What content already performed on a topic, who amplified it, and the questions audiences ask.
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com) - The questions people type into search engines around a keyword.
- [AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com) - "People also ask" question trees, useful for structuring a piece.
- [Feedly](https://feedly.com) - Follow competitor blogs and industry publications in one reader.
- [Google Alerts](https://www.google.com/alerts) - Free email alerts when a term appears in indexed news and pages.
- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) - What a technical audience is discussing today.
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) - Daily launches, a steady source of things to react to.

## Keyword and Search Research

- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - Keyword research, backlink analysis, content explorer and site audits.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) - Keyword, competitor and topic research with content planning tools.
- [Moz](https://moz.com) - Keyword research, rank tracking and link analysis.
- [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) - Free search volume and forecast data from Google Ads.
- [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) - Keyword ideas, content ideas and site audits with a free tier.
- [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com) - Browser extension showing search volume and CPC inside Google.

## Writing and Editing

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - General-purpose writing assistant; strongest when the brief is specific and dated.
- [Claude](https://claude.ai) - Writing assistant with a long context window, good for long-form drafts and rewrites.
- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) - AI writing platform for marketing teams with brand voice and campaign workflows.
- [Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai) - AI copy generation with workflows for marketing and sales content.
- [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com) - AI writer for articles, ads and landing pages.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - Grammar, clarity and tone checks inside the tools you already write in.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com) - Free readability checker that flags long sentences and passive voice.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com) - Docs, wikis and databases; where many teams draft and keep the content calendar.

## Content Optimization

- [Clearscope](https://www.clearscope.io) - Content grading against top-ranking pages for a target keyword.
- [Surfer](https://surferseo.com) - On-page optimization guidance with a content editor and audit.
- [MarketMuse](https://www.marketmuse.com) - Topic modeling and content planning at the site level.
- [Frase](https://www.frase.io) - Research, outlines and optimization for search-driven articles.

- [WordPress](https://wordpress.org) - Open-source CMS behind a large share of company blogs.
- [Ghost](https://ghost.org) - Open-source publishing with newsletters and memberships built in.
- [Webflow](https://webflow.com) - Visual site builder with a CMS for blogs and resource hubs.
- [Framer](https://www.framer.com) - Design-led site builder with a CMS and hosting.
- [Medium](https://medium.com) - Publishing platform with a built-in audience for essays meant to travel.
- [DEV Community](https://dev.to) - Publishing platform and community for developer content.
- [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) - Developer blogging on your own domain with a shared feed.

## Books

- [Everybody Writes](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Everybody+Writes+Ann+Handley) - By Ann Handley. A practical guide to writing content people want to read.
- [Content Inc.](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Content+Inc+Joe+Pulizzi) - By Joe Pulizzi. Building an audience first and a business on top of it.
- [They Ask, You Answer](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=They+Ask+You+Answer+Marcus+Sheridan) - By Marcus Sheridan. Answering buyer questions honestly as a content strategy.
- [Building a StoryBrand](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Building+a+StoryBrand+Donald+Miller) - By Donald Miller. A messaging framework that makes the customer the hero.
- [Made to Stick](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Made+to+Stick+Chip+Heath) - By Chip Heath and Dan Heath. Why some ideas survive and others die.
- [Contagious](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Contagious+Jonah+Berger) - By Jonah Berger. The science of why things catch on.
- [The Content Fuel Framework](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Content+Fuel+Framework+Melanie+Deziel) - By Melanie Deziel. A system for generating content ideas on demand.
